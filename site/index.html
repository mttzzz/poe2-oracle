Fast and light

PoE2 Oracle is a native Windows app written in Rust, with no Electron and no browser inside. It's one process: the graphics card draws its panels and overlays through Direct3D 11, with GPUI, the UI framework of the Zed code editor, and DirectWrite draws the text. The only browser it ever opens is the optional sign-in window for pathofexile.com, which is Windows' own WebView2 and closes with the window.

While you play, that leaves your memory to the game: Task Manager shows one process of about . Nothing else starts with it, and there's nothing else to install.

Next to other price checkers

Exiled Exchange 2 turns off hardware acceleration and draws with the CPU, so it holds no GPU memory; Overwolf's overlay in the game runs inside the game's own process and isn't counted.

PoE Overlay II runs only on Overwolf, so Overwolf's processes count as its own. Started from its desktop shortcut, it opened its main window by itself, with two animated banner ads and an offer of Premium to remove them. While that window stays open, Overwolf's built-in browser keeps redrawing it and uses more than a whole CPU core; minimized, the app needs about half the memory.

What it does

PoE2 Oracle runs in the background. The XP overlay is always in view: it sits on the game's own HUD, looks like part of it and can be turned off in the settings. The price panel opens over the game only when you press the hotkey. Prices need no account and no browser.

Price check

The item's modifiers become search filters with their tiers. Pick how strictly to search, adjust the bounds, search by the whole item class or just the base, and see the cheapest listings with their price, seller and age.

  • Searches the official trade site, the English or the Russian one to match your client
  • Tiers, pseudo totals, weapon DPS and defences as filters
  • Hover a listing to see it as the game's item tooltip, its modifiers compared with yours
  • For a seller who trades in person, a click copies the whisper; you send it yourself. Instant Buyout listings, searched by default, need none

Craft of Exile in one click, from either client

The Craft of Exile ↗ link on the price panel opens the item in Craft of Exile's crafting simulator with its base, item level, rarity, implicits and mods already set, with nothing to copy or paste. Craft of Exile's own import reads only the English client's text: a copy from the Russian client loses every mod. PoE2 Oracle hands the item over by the game's own ids instead, so it arrives the same from the Russian client as from the English one.

  • For normal, magic and rare gear, flasks, charms, jewels and waystones; uniques, currency, gems, relics, tablets and unidentified items get no link
  • A mod whose id isn't certain is left out, never guessed
  • With the Russian interface, Craft of Exile opens in Russian

XP overlay

Two plates right on the game's HUD rails, drawn in its own moulding and colours. Above the flask panel: how fast you gain experience and when the next level comes. Above the skill panel: a timer for the current map.

  • Levelling speed in percent of a level per hour, and the time to the next level at that speed
  • Time and experience of the current map, and your average map time for the session
  • Time in towns and hideouts stays out of the speed: while you pause, the line dims and shows only how much of the level you have
  • Experience is read from the XP bar on screen and from the game's log

Currency market

Currency, omens, runes, essences and everything else traded on the in-game Currency Exchange are priced from GGG's own record of the trades made on it: the value in divine, exalted and chaos orbs from the latest hour of trading (up to three for a thinly traded item), hourly volume and what your whole stack is worth, with poe2scout's seven-day chart beside it. Unique items, and exchange items that haven't traded lately, show poe2scout's price.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit
  • Path of Exile 2 with the English or Russian client
  • The game in Windowed or Windowed Fullscreen mode: nothing can be shown over exclusive fullscreen
  • An internet connection for prices

Getting started

  1. Download the latest installer, PoE2-Oracle-Setup-<version>.exe.
  2. Run it. The installer isn't code-signed yet: if Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. No administrator rights are needed.
  3. In the game's graphics options, choose Windowed Fullscreen.
  4. Point at an item in the game and press Ctrl+E.

Full install guide

The app speaks English and Russian. At first it follows the game client's language, or Windows' until the game has run; Interface language in the settings changes it.
