PoE2 Oracle

Price check for Path of Exile 2 on a hotkey. Point at an item, press Ctrl+E, and a panel next to your inventory shows what similar items sell for on the official trade site.

Download for Windows Read the guide

Free and open source · Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit · English and Russian game clients

What it does

PoE2 Oracle runs in the background and draws over the game only when you ask it to. Prices need no account and no browser.

Price check

The item's modifiers become search filters with their tiers. Pick how strictly to search, adjust the bounds, search by the whole item class or just the base, and see the cheapest listings with their price, seller and age.

  • Searches the official trade site, the English or the Russian one to match your client
  • Tiers, pseudo totals, weapon DPS and defences as filters
  • Hover a listing to see it as the game's item tooltip, its modifiers compared with yours
  • A click copies the whisper to the seller; you send it yourself

Currency market

Currency, omens, runes, essences and everything else traded on the in-game Currency Exchange are priced from GGG's own record of the trades made on it: the value in divine, exalted and chaos orbs for the last hour, a seven-day chart, hourly volume and what your whole stack is worth. Unique items, and exchange items that haven't traded lately, show poe2scout's price.

XP overlay

On top of the game's own HUD: experience per hour and the time to the next level above the flask panel, the time and experience of the current map next to your average map time above the skill panel.

Settings and quick actions

Pick the league, the hotkey, the default sellers and the interface scale. Quick actions put chat commands such as /hideout or a stash search on a hotkey of your choice.

  • Waystone marksMark waystone modifiers as danger, caution or wanted and spot them on every waystone you check.
  • Live searchSigned in to pathofexile.com, watch a search: each new listing arrives as a card over the game.
  • UpdatesNew versions come from the project's GitHub releases and are installed only after their SHA-256 checksum matches.
  • Problem reportsOne click saves a diagnostics report and opens a GitHub form with the details filled in. The app sends nothing itself.
  • PrivacyNo telemetry; signing in is optional. It talks only to the Path of Exile trade site and GGG's server of pictures and exchange data, poe2scout and GitHub. Details

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit
  • Path of Exile 2 with the English or Russian client
  • The game in Windowed or Windowed Fullscreen mode: nothing can be shown over exclusive fullscreen
  • An internet connection for prices

Getting started

  1. Download PoE2-Oracle-Setup-<version>.exe from the latest release.
  2. Run it. The installer is not code-signed yet: if Windows SmartScreen warns, click More info, then Run anyway. No administrator rights are needed.
  3. In the game's graphics options, choose Windowed Fullscreen.
  4. Point at an item in the game and press Ctrl+E.

Full install guide

The app speaks English and Russian. At first it follows the game client's language; Interface language in the settings changes it.
